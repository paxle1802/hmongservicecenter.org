Who we are

A trusted home base for the Hmong community

Founded in 1984, the Hmong Service Center, Inc. supports Hmong families as they overcome barriers in economic stability, education, language, and leadership. We are a familiar, culturally-grounded place families can turn to — and a bridge that connects them to the wider community's services and opportunities.

What we do

Practical help for everyday life

Our work centers on the real barriers families face — and on connecting people to the help they are entitled to.

🤝

Family & Economic Support

Guidance with employment, benefits, and financial stability so families can stand on solid ground.

📚

Education & Youth

School navigation, tutoring, and scholarships that help young people succeed and stay connected.

💬

Language & Interpretation

English–Hmong interpretation, translation, and help completing forms and paperwork.

🧭

Resource Navigation

We connect families to healthcare, housing, and social services across the Fox Valley.

Alongside these services, we help preserve Hmong language and heritage so the next generation stays rooted in who they are.
